My name is Corné, and I'm a freelance [software developer](https://github.com/cornedriesprong/), currently working remotely for [Audiomovers](https://audiomovers.com). I've been programming professionally for over a decade, and I have experience in Swift, C, Objective-C, C++, Rust and JavaScript/TypeScript (front- and backend web development). I'm particularly interested in music technology, and have developed two iOS/iPadOS music apps: [cykle](https://cp3.io/cykle) and [polybeat](https://cp3.io/polybeat).

In my free time I like to [make music](https://soundcloud.com/sleeprites).
